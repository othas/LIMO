The use of AI in Linear Models

In short: you may use AI in this course, you remain responsible for everything you hand in, and at the exam you must be able to do it all without AI.

1. The UHasselt regulations apply

The general UHasselt regulations on the use of AI apply to this course at all times. In particular, you must acknowledge your use of AI, and you remain accountable for the correctness of your work. The rules below come on top of these regulations and never replace them.

2. While studying: allowed, and best learned step by step

Within the UHasselt regulations you are free to use AI when studying this course. We advise you to learn this gradually and to follow the AI steps in the Learning Path. They start with using AI to explain and to check, and build up towards using AI as an assistant in a data analysis.

However you use AI, you must also be able to master the course without it. An explanation or an analysis that you cannot reproduce or defend yourself is not yet yours.

3. Homework assignments: allowed, with restraint

You may use AI for the homework assignments. We recommend that you do not rely on it too much, unless the assignment explicitly asks you to use AI. The assignments are your practice for the exam, and that practice only works if the thinking is your own.

For every assignment:

  • state where and how you used AI, as the UHasselt regulations require;
  • check everything the AI produced. Errors in your work are your errors, whatever their source.

4. The final exam: no AI

At the final exam you are not allowed to use AI in any way. Prepare for this from the first week: make sure that you can do the analyses, the interpretation and the reasoning yourself.
