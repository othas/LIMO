  4. AIGetting ready to work with AI

    In this course you gradually learn to use an AI assistant: first to help you study, later to help you analyse data. You are not expected to master this now; that is the goal at the end of the course. For now: (1) read the rules of this course on the use of AI; (2) choose the AI assistant that you will use; (3) start an AI logbook, a simple file in which you note for every AI step what you asked, what you got, and what you found when you checked it. One rule holds for the whole course: the AI assists, you are responsible. Never hand in or rely on something that you cannot explain yourself.
